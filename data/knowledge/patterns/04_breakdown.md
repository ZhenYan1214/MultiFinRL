# Breakdown

## Pattern
A breakdown occurs when price closes clearly below a support level, such as the bottom of a trading range, a prior swing low, or a neckline. On a short window the latest candles fall below the lows that held earlier in the window, and the latest close sits at or near the bottom of the range.

## Volume
Breakdowns are often accompanied by rising volume, although a decline can also continue on moderate volume once support gives way.

## Price-volume reading
A breakdown on expanding volume shows heavy selling and is more likely to continue, with the old support turning into resistance. A breakdown on light volume that quickly reverses back into the range may be a false break.
