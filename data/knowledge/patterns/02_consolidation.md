# Consolidation

## Pattern
Consolidation is a sideways range in which price moves between a roughly flat support level and a roughly flat resistance level without making progress in either direction. Candles overlap heavily, highs and lows stay within a band, and the latest close sits somewhere inside that band.

## Volume
Volume usually contracts during a consolidation as buyers and sellers wait. Isolated spikes near the edges of the range show tests of support or resistance.

## Price-volume reading
A quiet range on shrinking volume is a pause that often resolves in the direction of the prior trend. The range ends when price closes outside it; a move out of the range on expanding volume is more reliable than one on light volume.
