# Uptrend

## Pattern
An uptrend is a series of rising swing highs and rising swing lows. Each pullback stops above the previous low, and each rally carries price to a new high. On a short window the candles drift from the lower left of the chart to the upper right, with green candles outnumbering red ones and the latest close sitting in the upper part of the range.

## Volume
In a healthy uptrend, volume tends to expand on up days and contract on pullbacks. A steady or rising volume profile during the advance shows that buyers keep participating.

## Price-volume reading
Rising price on rising volume confirms the trend. Rising price on shrinking volume suggests the advance is losing momentum and may stall or turn into a range. A sudden volume spike on a down day inside an uptrend is an early warning of distribution.
