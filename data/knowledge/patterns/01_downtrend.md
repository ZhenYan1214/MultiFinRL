# Downtrend

## Pattern
A downtrend is a series of falling swing highs and falling swing lows. Rebounds fail below the previous high, and each decline pushes price to a new low. On a short window the candles drift from the upper left to the lower right, red candles dominate, and the latest close sits in the lower part of the range.

## Volume
Volume often expands on down days and contracts on rebounds. Heavy volume during the decline shows active selling pressure.

## Price-volume reading
Falling price on rising volume confirms the downtrend. Falling price on drying-up volume suggests selling is exhausting and a base may form. A high-volume reversal day near the lows can mark capitulation, but the trend is only broken once a rebound clears a prior swing high.
