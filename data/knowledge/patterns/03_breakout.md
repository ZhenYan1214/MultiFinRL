# Breakout

## Pattern
A breakout occurs when price closes clearly above a resistance level, such as the top of a trading range, a prior swing high, or a neckline. On a short window the latest candles push above the highs that capped price earlier in the window, and the latest close sits at or near the top of the range.

## Volume
A valid breakout is usually accompanied by a clear increase in volume on the breakout day or days, often well above the recent average.

## Price-volume reading
A breakout on expanding volume shows strong demand and is more likely to hold, with the old resistance turning into support. A breakout on flat or shrinking volume is suspect and may be a false breakout that falls back into the range.
